Services

  • PLC and robot programming

    Machine control code written for clarity and for the people who will maintain it. I work in Rockwell Studio 5000 on ControlLogix platforms, including Kinetix servo motion, and I program industrial robots to fit the cell and the cycle.

  • Controls standards and code libraries

    Standard objects, naming conventions and program structure that make machines look and behave alike. A good standard shortens build time, speeds up troubleshooting and makes new engineers productive sooner.

  • HMI development

    Operator screens that show what the machine is doing and what to do next. Consistent layouts, clear alarms and diagnostics that help maintenance find a fault without calling the programmer.

  • Commissioning and troubleshooting support

    Help getting a machine through debug, buyoff and startup, or tracking down a problem on one that is already running. I work from the code, the drawings and the machine itself.

  • Process and quality improvement

    Review of build and test practices, documentation and quality systems on the controls side of a project. The goal is fewer surprises at commissioning and less rework after shipment.

About

I'm Jordan Rogers, a controls and robotics engineer in Columbia, SC. I have more than ten years of experience on custom automation machines, working with OEMs and systems integrators on equipment for automotive and industrial manufacturers.

I hold BS and MS degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of South Carolina. My day-to-day work covers PLC and robot programming, HMI development, controls standards, commissioning and troubleshooting.

Rogers Automation Co. is a small independent practice. You work directly with me, and I take on a limited number of projects at a time.
